I set up assistants for people who aren't technical. Each one runs in its own container with its own logins, so no assistant can see into another person's.

One sends a small business owner a list of the day's invoices each evening. Only that person's yes sends them to the accountant, and the accountant's address is fixed in code.

Another reads a restaurant's work phone. It sorts what came in and drafts replies, but it can't send on that number. The owner copies each draft and sends it.

The rule they share: the people who use them only hear outcomes in plain words. Anything technical comes to me, without their personal data.
